Fixed phantom STT TTFB metrics: a TTFB clock armed by an utterance that produced no transcript (e.g. a VAD false-start) survived its utterance and was stopped by the next utterance's transcript, reporting multi-second values. New VAD speech-starts and no-show expiry now discard the armed clock, and the no-show window is derived per provider as `max(stt_ttfb_timeout, ttfs_p99_latency * 1.5)` so slow-but-real finals are never cut off mid-flight.
