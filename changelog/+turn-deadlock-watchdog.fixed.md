Fixed a user-turn deadlock: with standard (VAD/transcription) turn strategies, the aggregator's own synthetic `UserStartedSpeakingFrame` broadcast could echo back into the `UserTurnController` and set `_user_speaking` stale-true, permanently disarming the `user_turn_stop_timeout` watchdog — a turn re-opened by a late transcript (with VAD-gated stop strategies unable to fire without VAD activity) then starved the conversation until the user spoke again. `UserStarted/StoppedSpeakingFrame` now drive `_user_speaking` only with `ExternalUserTurnStrategies`, where they are the authoritative speech signal; the watchdog's forced stop pushes any accumulated aggregation so the recovered turn still answers.
